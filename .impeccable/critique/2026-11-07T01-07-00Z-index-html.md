## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM Assessment:** Emerging & Fragmented (6/10). The transit motif (*Biyahe* — *"Kasya pa, sakay na!"*) and Geodetic Engineering metaphors (*Survey Control Network*) establish a distinctive, authentic core identity. However, they currently sit as a decorative skin over generic web dashboard widgets. Structural interactions rely on MMORPG mechanics (AppCoins, pots, rerolls) rather than authentic collegiate transit waypoints.

**Deterministic Scan (Assessment B):** 22 findings detected across `index.html` (14 Quality, 8 Slop; 12 Warnings, 10 Advisories).
- **Critical Accessibility Failure:** Low contrast warning (1.7:1 vs required 4.5:1) for `#FBB03B` text on `#FFF7EB` (`#timeInMemberBadge` and `#activeTambayBanner`).
- **Layout & Structure:** Cramped padding in `.table-responsive` with zero container inset; 6 nested card instances (`.card` inside `.card`) creating visual clutter in settings and perks.
- **Hierarchy:** Semantic skipped heading level (`<h2>` followed directly by `<h4>` in inspection modal).
- **Design System Drift:** 9 ad-hoc font-size rem values (`0.65rem`–`1.6rem`) off DESIGN.md ramp; undocumented hex color `#9e2a2b` on delete button.
- **False Positives Confirmed:** Detector flagged `cream-palette` (`#F4F1EB`) and `overused-font` (`Fraunces`), which are confirmed, deliberate brand commitments documented in `PRODUCT.md` and `DESIGN.md`.

## Overall Impression

The portal has a passionate, culturally authentic heart: the concept of fusing geodetic surveying triangulation with Filipino jeepney transit (*Biyahe*) is brilliant. However, the user experience currently stumbles on three fronts: an intimidating, anxiety-inducing landing state ("0% Grade" in alarm red), severe mobile scroll-trapping by the 450px network map, and high-risk administrative ergonomics (an unprotected delete button and repetitive manual verification loops).

## What's Working

1. **Tactile Paper Surfaces & Typography Pairing:** The combination of chiseled Fraunces numerals and clean Figtree body on warm Manila parchment (`#F4F1EB`) creates a collegiate notebook aesthetic that feels authentic and non-corporate.
2. **Survey Control Network Concept:** Visualizing applicants and resident members as interconnected benchmark stations and survey nodes through Vis.js is a standout domain metaphor.
3. **Dual Verification Fallback:** Pairing visual QR codes with simple 6-character alphanumeric fallback codes ensures resilient campus usability even on spotty Wi-Fi and older mobile devices.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] Threatening "0% Grade" & Crimson Progress Bar on Applicant Landing**
  - **Why it matters:** Greeting anxious applicants on Day 1 with an empty red progress bar and "0%" triggers academic dread and imposter syndrome instead of motivating progress.
  - **Fix:** Replace the raw 0% grade bar with an encouraging milestone journey tracker ("Station 1: Onboarding Complete"). Relocate the 7-pillar grade calculation to an expandable drawer with reassuring progress colors.
  - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

- **[P1] Mobile Scroll Trap & Cognitive Congestion on Overview Viewport**
  - **Why it matters:** The Overview tab stacks 6 major widgets. The 450px Vis.js canvas captures mobile swipe gestures, zooming the network into the void instead of scrolling the page.
  - **Fix:** Move the Survey Control Network to a dedicated tab or collapsible drawer. Keep the Overview tab focused on a "Next Action" hero card, key stats, and immediate buddy tasks. Add `touch-action: pan-y` fencing.
  - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`

- **[P1] High-Risk Unconfirmed Destructive Action in Admin Inspection Modal**
  - **Why it matters:** The red "Delete" button sits flush beside routine export actions in `#adminInspectionModal` with zero confirmation modal, creating an extreme risk of accidental data loss on mobile.
  - **Fix:** Isolate "Delete" into a collapsed "Danger Zone" section requiring a 2-step verification modal where the officer types the applicant's nickname.
  - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

- **[P2] Toxic Crowdfunding Dynamics & AC Scarcity in Perks Shop**
  - **Why it matters:** Crowdfunding group task deadline extensions using personal AppCoins creates peer pressure, social shaming, and transactional friction among buddy group members.
  - **Fix:** Convert deadline extensions from individual coin donations to collective group milestone achievements (e.g., unlocked when 100% of buddy group members log their weekly tambay).
  - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable shape`

- **[P2] Severe Token Dissonance & Inline Styling Drift**
  - **Why it matters:** Over 100 inline `style=""` blocks override design tokens, break dark mode adaptability, and trigger contrast failures (1.7:1).
  - **Fix:** Extract inline styles into semantic CSS classes in `styles.css`; normalize font sizes to DESIGN.md tokens; resolve contrast on `--brand-mint`.
  - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan (First-Time Applicant):**
  - Confronted by a red "0% Grade" badge on Day 1 with zero guidance or onboarding glossary.
  - Trapped by the 450px network map when trying to scroll down on mobile.
  - Feels social guilt visiting the Shop with 0 AC while peers crowdfund an extension pot.

- **Alex (RAComm Officer):**
  - In danger of accidentally tapping "Delete" instead of "Export PDF" while reviewing applicants on a phone.
  - Must scroll through a cramped, un-insetLayout table without status filter pills ("At Risk", "Complete").

- **Sam (Resident Member):**
  - Endures a tedious 5-step modal loop to verify each applicant at a noisy Melchor Hall tambayan, causing verification queues.
  - Has no view of remaining signatory traits without asking officers.

## Minor Observations

1. Header background (`#0d0d0d`) feels overly heavy and abrupt against the Manila parchment canvas (`#F4F1EB`).
2. When2Meet schedule grid compresses 8 columns into mobile viewports without freeze columns, making touch selection difficult.
3. Alphanumeric shortcode copy button lacks a visual "Copied!" confirmation tooltip.
4. Dark mode has inconsistent coverage due to hardcoded inline colors.

## Questions to Consider

1. *What if the Overview tab replaced the numerical grade percentage entirely until mid-recruitment, framing progress instead as "Stops Reached" along the Biyahe route?*
2. *How might the Survey Control Network transform from an abstract node canvas into an interactive campus map anchoring benchmark control stations across Melchor Hall and Sunken Garden?*
3. *Could resident verification be elevated from a manual modal into a continuous QR camera scanner or a 1-tap "Passport Stamp" interaction?*
